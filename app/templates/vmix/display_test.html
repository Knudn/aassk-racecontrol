URL Params

textThe text to display
sizeFont size (e.g. 32, 2rem, 5vw)
colorColor name or hex (e.g. red, %23ff6600)
fontserif, sans, mono, cursive, fantasy
bgBackground color
alignleft, center, right
bold1 to enable bold
italic1 to enable italic
Example:
?text=Hello+World&size=48&color=%23e74c3c&font=serif&bold=1
